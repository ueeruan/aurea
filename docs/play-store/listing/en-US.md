## Descrição completa (1840/4000)

```
Aurea is a video and motion graphics editor built for phones. Its own graphics engine renders the preview and the export on your device's GPU, so you can build layered compositions, animate with keyframes and mix video, 2D, 3D, text and effects in one project.

TWO WAYS TO EDIT
• Composition: layers, keyframes, curve editor, masks, blend modes, nulls and precompositions
• Editing: a main track with cuts in sequence, centered playhead, transitions between cuts and audio waveforms
• Switch interfaces anytime, in the same project

3D IN YOUR COMPOSITION
• GLB, glTF, OBJ and FBX models with textures
• 3D text, 3D shapes, lights, 3D camera and HDRI lighting

200+ GPU EFFECTS
• Glow, light rays, blurs, glitch, distortions, color, particles, Drawing Progress and much more
• Effect browser with before/after previews, favorites and recents

TEXT AND CAPTIONS
• Animated 2D and 3D text presets
• Auto captions generated on the device, in several languages

HEAVY VIDEOS
• 4K and high frame rate videos get a light edit copy so editing stays smooth; export uses the original

AI
• Person and object cutout, depth maps and upscaling processed on the device
• AI Video (text or image to video): optional and online

EXPORT
• MP4 in H.264 or HEVC, up to 4K and 60 fps depending on the device
• If the device can't handle it, Aurea adjusts by itself and exports in a compatible mode
• PNG, PNG sequence and GIF, with transparent background

COMMUNITY
• Publish projects and presets, like, comment and follow other creators
• Report content and block profiles

LANGUAGES
Português, English, Español, Русский, Bahasa Indonesia and العربية.

GOOD TO KNOW
• The app is free and shows ads.
• The account is optional: it is used for the Community and AI Video.
• Requires Android 8.0 or later. Heavy 3D or 4K projects need a device with more memory.
```
