Modified for AUREA: the image encoder and the mask decoder of the published
checkpoint efficienttam_ti_512x512.pt were rewritten for export (attention as
plain matrix products, shape-only tables as constants) and converted to ncnn
with pnnx, weights in fp16. The recipe is in tools/efficienttam/.
Training weights were not changed. Runtime inference is float32 on CPU.
The memory encoder and memory attention are not shipped: this model selects the
object on the clicked frame, and the Rotobrush propagation follows it.

efficienttam-ti-prompt.bin holds, as little-endian float32, the prompt encoder
Gaussian matrix (2x128, scaled by 2 pi), its label table (5x256), the "no mask"
embedding (256) and the "no memory" embedding (256).
