AUREA Rotobrush local foreground model

U2-Net / U2NETP, Xuebin Qin and contributors.
Source: https://github.com/xuebinqin/U-2-Net
License: Apache-2.0, included as LICENSE-U2Net.txt.
Paper: U2-Net: Going Deeper with Nested U-Structure for Salient Object Detection.

The ONNX conversion is distributed by the rembg project:
https://github.com/danielgatis/rembg/releases/download/v0.0.0/u2netp.onnx
Original ONNX MD5: 8e83ca70e441ab06c318d82300c84806.

Modified for AUREA: converted ONNX to ncnn at 320x320 with pnnx 20260526,
then compressed weights to fp16 with ncnnoptimize (flag 65536).
Training weights were not changed. Runtime inference is float32 on CPU.
The verified weights and graph are embedded in the shared Android/iOS core;
first use and reopening a project work without a model download.
The model estimates salient foreground. It is not Adobe Roto Brush and
does not provide SAM-style arbitrary-object prompting or video tracking.
